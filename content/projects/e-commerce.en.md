## The project

A major university project: building a marketplace web app where sellers and buyers meet, done by a team of five over three months.

## My role

I worked as a **developer and analyst**:

- helping build the app's features,
- *troubleshooting* features that broke,
- writing the **project documentation**.

## What I learned

Working in a team of five means code, decisions and documentation have to make sense to other people, not only to yourself.
