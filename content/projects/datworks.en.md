## The client

**datworks** is a photography and videography service from West Java (pre-wedding, weddings, graduations, events and products). They needed an online portfolio to show their work to future clients.

## First version (2025)

This was a university assignment and a freelance job at the same time, done by two of us. I **ran the project from requirements to handover**, and talked with the client almost every day to agree on the content and the look.

The site was built with WordPress (a page builder) on Hostinger. It was **approved by the client and used as their online portfolio**.

## Rebuilt (2026)

I never kept the old version in a repository, so there was no code to show. I rebuilt it from scratch as a static, "Darkroom" themed website:

- a preloader that "develops" a photo from negative to positive with WebGL,
- galleries per category that can be filtered,
- series pages with their own layouts,
- unit and end-to-end tests.

The code is in a public GitHub repository, and the site is live at the demo link above.

## What I learned

Working directly with a client taught me to agree on the requirements early. Rebuilding it taught me to keep every piece of work in a repository.
