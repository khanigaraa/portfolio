## Learning to stay calm at rush hour

While studying, I worked at Ekara Coffee & Eatery in Ciumbuleuit, Bandung. I started as a part-time waiter on the busiest days and moved up step by step: mobile staff, head of bar, and finally **stock manager for four outlets**.

That taught me two things that turned out to matter a lot in IT: staying calm when everything moves fast, and **being careful with data**. As stock manager I reconciled purchases against usage and actual stock until the discrepancies were zero.

## Servers that must not go down

At university I fell for infrastructure. For my thesis I moved an educational institution's information system from a single server to a **three-node high availability cluster** with Galera Cluster, Keepalived and Nginx over a VXLAN network. When a node is shut down the service keeps running, with a measured availability of 99.95%.

## Websites for real people

I also build websites for clients, such as Datworks, the portfolio of a photography business. I ran it from requirements to handover and talked with the client almost every day.

I am looking for a job in infrastructure, systems or the web, and I am open to freelance projects. If you need someone who can keep servers running and build websites people enjoy using, let's talk.
